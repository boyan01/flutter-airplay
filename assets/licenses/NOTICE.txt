The Android playback implementation includes source derived from
jqssun/android-airplay-server, GPL-3.0-only, commit
c8defdd70d7e6a04f4f1b71d353653682d594106.
https://github.com/jqssun/android-airplay-server
Copyright holders are retained in upstream LICENSE and source notices.
Copied paths and pinned sources appear in dependencies.lock.json.
Changes: JNI host replaced to fit pinned UxPlay 1.73.7, Flutter embedded texture
and explicit NSD lifecycle; VideoRenderer error/output callbacks added.
Copied renderer classes use the tech.soit.flutterairplay.renderer namespace;
source notices and pinned upstream paths preserve provenance.

UxPlay is maintained directly in vendor/UxPlay; its upstream base is recorded
in vendor/UxPlay/UPSTREAM.md. GPL-3.0-or-later UxPlay,
LGPL-2.1-or-later libplist, Apache-2.0 BoringSSL, PlayFair and llhttp notices are
packaged under assets/licenses. See android/CORE_NOTICE.md.

ships on Windows and Linux, are in lychee_player-NOTICES.txt.
